Bring an existing world with you

Open your previous HTML game in the browser that holds its save. Press J → Export backup. In this local-server version, use J → Import backup and choose that JSON file. Import while you are the only connected player. A before-import copy is saved automatically on the host.

If your GeForce card is not listed

Enable graphics acceleration in your browser and restart it. On Windows, use Settings → System → Display → Graphics to set your browser to the high-performance GPU, then restart the browser. The launcher requests this GPU but cannot override your operating-system choice.

What these profiles mean

Profiles set rendering budgets; they do not detect or reserve exactly 4 or 8 GB of VRAM. Frame rate depends on resolution, world complexity and CPU as well as the GPU. Automatic resolution adjustment remains available. Shader ray tracing runs on the GPU's shader cores; hardware RTX, DLSS and frame generation are not included.
